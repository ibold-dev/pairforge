# PairForge domain model

PairForge is a policy-driven launchpad for paired assets. Its central artifact is
a **preset manifest**: a versioned, validated description of how a token moves
from DBC discovery into DAMM v2 liquidity.

## Core concepts

| Concept                | Meaning                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Preset                 | A reusable launch policy such as Catalyst, Conviction, or Pair Thesis.             |
| Preset version         | An immutable revision of one preset, identified by a deterministic digest.         |
| Curve policy           | Intent, timing, and the trading-fee schedule for the DBC phase.                    |
| Curve fee split        | The distribution of fees earned while the virtual pool is active.                  |
| Migration policy       | The graduation objective and DAMM v2 migration choice that end the DBC phase.      |
| Liquidity allocation   | Who receives or locks DAMM v2 liquidity after graduation.                          |
| Launch draft           | The creator-specific inputs and recipient addresses bound to a preset version.     |
| Configuration manifest | The reproducible record of SDK version, policy, addresses, and resulting accounts. |

## Economic separation

Two fields answer different questions and are validated independently:

1. **Curve-phase fee split** answers who receives the fees generated by DBC
   swaps. Its shares must total 10,000 basis points.
2. **Graduated liquidity allocation** answers who can claim or must keep DAMM v2
   liquidity after migration. Its shares also total 10,000 basis points, and at
   least 10% remains locked under the initial product policy.

Neither policy implies the other. A recipient may participate in one, both, or
neither, subject to a complete launch draft.

## First preset family

- **Catalyst**: a time-bounded policy for attention-driven releases, with a
  measured early fee decay and a reachable graduation objective.
- **Conviction**: a longer discovery policy for launches that prioritize gradual
  participation and durable liquidity.
- **Pair Thesis**: a reference-aware policy for a token paired with an approved
  asset. It records reference context for disclosure; it does not continuously
  reprice an on-chain curve from an oracle.

All values remain declarative in Phase 1. A graduation objective is a product
target; the later SDK adapter selects the supported curve builder and derives the
actual protocol threshold when the selected builder requires it. SDK-backed
transaction composition, quote-asset catalogs, and network activity start in
later phases.
