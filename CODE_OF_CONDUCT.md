# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Use respectful and inclusive language.
- Assume good intent while giving specific, constructive feedback.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and project outcomes.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and the time of other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, or deliberate disruption of project discussion are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security or contact channel. Reports will be handled promptly and with appropriate discretion.
